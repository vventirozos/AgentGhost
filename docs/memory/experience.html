memory / experience.py — ExperienceMemory

How past owner turns like this one went — only the turns a human graded, rendered as steps and sources, never as answers.

Why it exists (§4QL, 2026-10-21)

The 10-21 answer census put knowledge answers at 29 % right, most failures being specifics invented on turns that called no tool. The one lesson that ever measurably changed an answer (§4ON) was the owner's "version" rule — in substance, go to the vendor's page: a memory of where the evidence came from, written as prose. The agent already held every owner turn with its tool calls (the trajectory corpus) and about 300 human labels for them (gold and census grades, the owner's 👍/👎), but nothing joined the two to retrieval. The unlabelled episode tier that used to hydrate prompts was measured 15 % relevant and 19 % harmful (§4NC) and retired; the 2026 literature on experience memory (ERL, MACLA, RIZZ, EvoMemBench) agrees it helps only with selective retrieval and outcome labels.

What a row is

One row per past owner turn with a human label: the request, the grade and the grader's reason, the tool steps it took (web_search("…") → browser(postgresql.org)), the sources that answered it (URLs the browser opened without error), the ones that failed (host plus the failure's own words, never the page's), and — only for a graded RIGHT answer to a question whose answer does not move — a short gist of the answer. Rows are built by ExperienceMemory.sync from the trajectory collector and the label files; the request's embedding (the vector store's own model) is kept beside the row, so similarity is a brute-force cosine over a few hundred vectors and the chroma collection is never touched.

A Slack requester's reaction may be a member's and is not used. Judge verdicts (verifier_late, the reaction judge) are never labels: they were wrong on 9 of 17 refusals on real traffic (§4OW). A thumb is coarser than a grade ("satisfied", not "every specific right"), so a thumb-labelled case renders its steps and sources but never its answer text.

Who is never indexed

A probe's turn, a member's turn, an undelivered reply, an ungraded turn, a request under two content words ("ok", "yes do it"), and any trajectory held out in the excluded table (an experiment's own cases, so a replay is leave-one-out).

What reaches a prompt

Nothing, in production: the tier is built and its index synced, but GHOST_EXPERIENCE_TIER is unset in the launcher (see Switches: the replay was inconclusive). With the switch on, on an owner turn the memory bus asks the store for cases like the request: identical wording always qualifies; otherwise cosine ≥ 0.85 on the user's own words (measured offline on 640 owner turns: at 0.80 "correct." matched "yes"). At most two lines render under ### EXPERIENCE — the closest RIGHT case (its steps, its sources, and the answer gist when allowed) and the closest bad case (WRONG before PARTIAL before FAILED-HONEST, with the grader's reason and "Do not repeat that"). The answer gist is withheld when the request is a moving target (the episode store's version/price rule) or time-bound (weather, news, today, now, a status question), when the turn was tool-driven (an execute/introspect chain), or when the label is a thumb.

Writers

One sync entry point, sync_experience_index, called by the idle store maintenance (at most every 6 h, with the graph prune and the reconcile) and by the operator's scripts/experience_sync_4ql.py (which also sets or clears the hold-out). The sync is idempotent: a row already present with the same label is left alone, a changed label rewrites the row, a withdrawn label or an excluded id removes it, and rows derived under an older renderer version (DERIVED_VERSION) are recomputed without re-embedding. The running agent re-reads the file when it changes on disk, so a hold-out set takes effect without a restart. The walk includes the 90-day trajectory archive (archive/<day>.tar.gz; battery R5b: a since_days walk silently dropped every graded turn older than the cut-off), a row is only removed when the walk SAW its trajectory unlabelled, and an insert is INSERT OR REPLACE so a second writer landing the same turn between the read and the write (the 6-hourly maintenance beside the script) does not abort the sync (R13).

Storage

SQLite at memory_dir/experience.db: table experience (tid PK, req_id, ts, request, request_norm, kind, grade, label_source, reason, steps, sources, failed_sources, answer, moving, vec, labelled_at, derived_v) and table excluded (tid, note). Everything in it is derived from the trajectory corpus and the label files and can be rebuilt by one sync.

Log lines

Boot: Experience Memory — N human-graded past turns indexed; OFF in prompts …. Per hydration the bus line carries the tier as x= (x=- when no store is wired, x=0 when it found nothing).
