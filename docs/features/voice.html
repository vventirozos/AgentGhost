Voice

Hold the mic, speak in Greek or English, and Ghost answers out loud. You can even talk over a reply to stop it or change course, and your voice never leaves your own network.

Runs on: Mac mini (nova) + Mac Studio (eva) No cloud speech service

What it does

Voice is built into the web console, not a separate app. Hold the mic button and speak; when you let go, the clip is turned into text and sent as an ordinary message. The language is detected per clip, so you can switch between Greek and English freely.

Replies can be read aloud. English is spoken by a natural-sounding voice (Phonon) on the Mac mini; Greek is spoken by the macOS Greek voice, because the Phonon voice reads English only.

The interesting part is that nothing here uses a hosted speech API. No account, no key, no recordings sent to a company. That is a hard rule for the agent, and the reason it is built this way.

How it works

  1. Record

    The browser records while you hold the mic. Its face “breathes” with your voice.

  2. Transcribe

    The clip goes through the console server to Whisper large-v3 on the Mac mini. Voice-activity detection cuts the silence before and after you speak, because Whisper tends to invent a phrase on silence.

  3. Answer

    The text is sent as a normal chat message, so everything Ghost can do in text it can do by voice.

  4. Speak

    The reply is split into chunks. Chunks written mostly in Greek letters go to the macOS Greek voice; the rest go to Phonon on the Mac mini. If the Mac mini does not answer, the macOS English voice takes over so the reply is never silent.

Talking over a reply

After a message you sent from the mic, the microphone keeps listening while the reply is spoken and for three seconds after. The mic button shows a ring while it does. A typed message never opens it. When it hears you, the reply pauses: real words stop it and are sent, noise or an empty transcript lets it continue. With loudspeakers the mic may hear the reply itself; after three false starts it stops listening for that reply and says so. A headset avoids this. Turn the feature off with Toggle voice interrupt in the command palette (⌘K).

The speaker button

A message sent from the mic is always spoken, even with the button off, and the button stays lit until your next typed message. Turning it on during a reply starts speech with the next reply. Reloading the page does not read out a reply already running.

Where it runs

The web console server on the Mac Studio (eva) receives the audio and passes it on. Speech-to-text (Whisper) and English speech (Phonon) run as services on the Mac mini (nova). The macOS say voices used for Greek and as the fallback run on the machine with the console server. Everything travels only over your private network.

The separate sentiment service on the Mac mini reads the tone of what you say (in English) and nudges Ghost's mood a little. It does not change the answer itself.

Limits and switches

  • Measured on read speech, Whisper gets about 1 word in 8 wrong in Greek and 1 in 20 in English, in about 3 seconds per clip. Noisy rooms do worse.
  • There is no fallback for speech-to-text: if Whisper is down, voice input fails rather than handing Ghost wrong words.
  • Phonon has no emotion or style control; tone is not carried into the voice.
  • Talk-over has been tested in the web browser; real rooms and iPhones are less tested.
  • Switches: GHOST_STT_URL (Whisper service), GHOST_TTS_URL (Phonon; empty means macOS say for everything), GHOST_TTS_VOICE and GHOST_TTS_VOICE_EL (the English and Greek say voices), GHOST_TTS_RATE.
