Feature

Memory

Closing the chat does not erase anything. Ghost keeps several kinds of memory, each good at a different question, and you can see, correct and delete what it holds.

Runs on: eva (Mac Studio) Tor-only

What it does

A single pile of searchable text is bad at ordinary questions. "What do I know about Postgres?", "Who is Markos to me?" and "What went wrong last time?" want different kinds of answer. So Ghost keeps separate stores:

  • Your profile: your name, city, work, preferences. It is not searched; it is written into every conversation, so Ghost never has to look it up.
  • Facts (vector recall): notes stored so they can be found by meaning, not exact words. Ask about "the database move" and it finds a note that said "migrating the schema".
  • Relationships (knowledge graph): who and what is connected to what, such as "Elena is my sister" or "the NetMon project runs on the Pi".
  • Temporal facts: facts with a time, so "I used to drive a Fiat, now a Volvo" keeps both and knows which is current.
  • The fact ledger: an append-only history. Each time a fact about you is stated, replaced or removed, one line records the date and the words you used. It lets Ghost answer "since when do you know where I live?".
  • Episodes: what happened, in order: asked X, tried Y, failed because Z, switched to W.
  • The journal: an inbox. Your turns queue here at once, and when things are quiet a background pass reads your own lines and files the facts you stated into the stores above.
  • The scratchpad: short notes for the current work, gone after a day.
  • Owner rules: instructions you dictated, shown on every one of your turns.

Try it

You sayWhat happens
“I moved to Thessaloniki last month.”The journal picks it up; your profile city is replaced (not added to), and the ledger records the change.
“Where do I live?”Answered from the profile, which is in every prompt.
“What did we decide about Kosmas's website?”Ghost calls recall and searches facts, episodes and past conversations by meaning.
“Actually my brother is Bruno, not Markos.”Single-valued facts are corrected in place.
“Learn this rule: always answer in English.”The system stores the sentence without a model call. It rides every one of your turns. At most ten rules.
“Show my rules” / “forget rule 2”The system lists them from the store. Forgetting shows what will go and needs “confirm forget rule 2” within 15 minutes.
“Forget everything about project Atlas.”Ghost shows a numbered list of what it found (files, facts, profile fields, graph facts, conversations) and deletes only what you confirm (“yes” or “only 1 and 3”).

How it works

  1. Write

    Your message goes to the journal straight away. After about two minutes of quiet, a model reads only your own lines, picks out the facts you stated, and files them. A question such as "do you remember my wife?" never counts as stating a fact.

  2. Prepare each turn

    Every turn carries your profile, the facts about you and your rules. For a question about yourself, matching facts are searched and added above the conversation ("hydration"). Lists from different stores are merged by rank position, not by raw score, because their scores are not comparable.

  3. Search on demand

    The rest (general facts, the graph, episodes, past conversations) is searched only when Ghost calls recall. Measured on real turns, adding them to every prompt helped little and sometimes harmed.

  4. Tidy when idle

    When nobody is talking to it, Ghost drains the journal and consolidates its stores.

Ghost does not invent lessons or rules for itself. The only learning is what you tell it: your facts and your rules. The old "proposed rules" step was removed, so "dismiss rule N" and "learn rule N" are now ordinary messages.

Not the same as memory: asking about one document you fed it ("in the Postgres manual, how do I…") uses knowledge_base query, which searches only that document. See Documents.

Where it runs

Everything lives on eva, as plain files under $GHOST_HOME/system/: the stores in system/memory/, past conversations in system/sessions/. There is no outside database. Backing up $GHOST_HOME backs up the agent's whole memory; deleting it makes Ghost forget you completely. The embedding model runs locally; nothing about you is sent to a keyed service.

Limits and switches

  • Something you said 30 seconds ago may not be findable yet; the journal drains after a quiet spell.
  • "It forgot" means one of two things: never stored, or stored but not found. Ask it to search for the fact directly first.
  • A wrong profile entry follows every turn until you correct it. The web console has a memory-correction control for stubborn ones.
  • At most ten owner rules; an eleventh is refused until you forget one.
  • Channel members do not read or write your private memory.
  • The fact ledger is always written but shown in prompts only behind a switch (off), and the experience store is off too (GHOST_EXPERIENCE_TIER).
  • Changing the embedding model makes Ghost refuse to start rather than return nonsense; a re-embed script exists for a real switch.
